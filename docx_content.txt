PROJECT 1
MASTER BUSINESS, PRODUCT & SOFTWARE BLUEPRINT
Travel Commerce + Membership + Referral + Reward Ecosystem
Version 1.0 — 11 September 2026
1. Executive Definition
Project 1 is a travel-commerce platform combined with a membership and referral ecosystem. The core business sells and supports genuine domestic and international travel. The member layer lets people share travel products through personal referral identities and, subject to the final compliant compensation plan, receive rewards connected to eligible travel transactions.
It is three connected products: (1) a premium public travel website, (2) a member portal, and (3) an admin/cloud control centre.
North Star: REAL TRAVEL → REAL BOOKINGS → REAL REVENUE → CONTROLLED, TRACEABLE REWARDS.

Membership
Working concept: ₹500 one-time membership. It creates a member account, Member ID, personal referral link and access to member tools. Direct referral rewards are tied to qualifying genuine travel transactions.
Active / Green travel status
A member becomes GREEN when they complete a qualifying personal travel booking under the final rules. Green represents an active travel/qualification cycle. The qualifying booking is displayed in the member portal. When the relevant travel cycle is completed, the status can expire/reset according to the approved policy.
The purpose is to keep higher-level participation connected to real travel activity rather than making one ₹500 payment an indefinite qualification.

8. Team / Upstream Rewards
Any upstream/team reward must be tied to a legitimate eligible travel transaction and the final compensation rules. Every reward record should contain source transaction, member, level, rule version, calculation and status.
The system must never depend on unexplained or manually invented balances.

32. Final Product Definition
Project 1 is a premium travel-commerce platform where customers discover and book real journeys, members share those journeys through a structured referral system, and secure cloud software tracks bookings, qualification, eligible rewards, wallets, payouts and business economics from one auditable system.
Excellent travel products and customer experience.
Beautiful premium public website.
Simple, enjoyable member portal.
Financially controlled and auditable reward engine.
Strong legal, consumer-protection, payment, privacy and tax compliance.
33. Compensation & Compliance — Mandatory Before Launch
The figures and concepts discussed in this project — including ₹500 membership, package price examples, direct rewards, team rewards, binary matching and Green activation — are working business assumptions, not a final legal compensation plan.
Before launch, the exact plan should be reviewed by qualified Indian legal/compliance professionals. In particular, review the Consumer Protection (Direct Selling) Rules, 2021, the Consumer Protection Act framework, the Prize Chits and Money Circulation Schemes (Banning) Act, 1978, applicable tax/payment rules, privacy requirements and advertising/consumer-protection claims.
The software should be configurable so that the final approved rules can be implemented without rebuilding the entire platform.
